How to Fix Android Phones That Become Slow After Installing a System Update

Tech on dicas covers practical Android guidance for users of Samsung, Motorola, and Xiaomi phones. When a device becomes sluggish just after a system update, checking whether background optimization has finished and whether apps need updates can help users avoid unnecessary resets while the system settles.

A phone that becomes slow immediately after a system update is experiencing a common and usually temporary condition. The update may have triggered background optimization processes, changed system resource requirements, or introduced compatibility issues with apps that have not yet been updated to match the new system version.

Post-Update Timeline

First 24 to 48 Hours

Background optimization runs automatically after major updates. The system reindexes files, optimizes apps, and rebuilds caches. During this period, the phone may feel slower than usual. This is normal and typically resolves without intervention.

After 48 Hours

If slowness persists beyond two days, background optimization is likely complete. The problem is now persistent rather than transitional and requires investigation. Check app updates — developers release compatibility patches after system updates.

Storage Check

System updates can consume significant storage space. If available storage dropped below 10 percent after the update, the slowness may be storage-related rather than optimization-related. Freeing space is the priority.

Safe Mode Test

If the phone is still slow, boot into Safe Mode to determine whether a third-party app is causing the problem. If performance improves in Safe Mode, an app compatibility issue is likely. Update or uninstall recently updated apps.

A factory reset should only be considered after all other checks have been exhausted and the phone has been slow for more than a week after the update. Most post-update slowness resolves within the first few days as the system and apps settle.
  • Background optimization after updates typically lasts 24 to 48 hours
  • App updates released after a system update fix compatibility-related slowness
  • Storage checks confirm whether the update consumed too much space
  • Safe Mode testing isolates app-specific problems from system-wide issues
